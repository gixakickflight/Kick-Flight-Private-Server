Patch 2.11.6

Release 2.11.6 Announcement

2026-11-02

Global chat is back, and up to 120 FPS.

Global chat is back

  • Open it from the chat (message) button on Home or from the lobby/matching screen.
  • Tabs: Chatting, Team, Friend and Beginner. All of them are open to everyone.
  • Send messages (up to 140 characters) and stamps. Tap a player's badge to open their profile.
  • The latest 100 messages of each tab are kept.

Higher frame rate

  • The 30 FPS cap is raised to up to 120 FPS. The actual FPS depends on your device's screen and performance.

Update required

  • Android clients on 2.11.5 and older can no longer connect.
  • It installs over 2.11.5 without uninstalling (same signature), so your data is kept.
