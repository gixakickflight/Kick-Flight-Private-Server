Patch 2.11.806

2026-11-09

The game is now Fenix. It is a new app that installs alongside the old one, and accounts do not carry over.

Important

  • Fenix (package world.global.fenix) is a new app: it installs alongside the old Kick Flight app, not over it.
  • Accounts do not carry over: everyone starts a new account. You can uninstall the old app afterwards.
  • Download Fenix-2.11.806.apk from the site. Older versions show the update screen, which opens the download page.

New

  • Battle ping counter under the enemy team, colour-coded. Turn it on or off in Settings › Battle (Ping HUD). The Wi-Fi warning icon was removed.
  • Matchmaking waits 60 seconds (20 s longer) for other players before filling with bots.
  • Release details: Android 2.11.806 announcement.

Patch 2.11.805

2026-11-08

The server moved to Europe and the game now connects through the domain kick-flight-fenix.us.ci.

Changed

  • The Fenix server now runs in Europe, which should lower latency for players in Europe.
  • The game connects to kick-flight-fenix.us.ci (API, chat and matches) instead of a fixed IP address, so future server moves will not require a new APK.

Update required

  • Android: update to 2.11.805 is required. Older versions point to the previous server, which is offline. Download Fenix-2.11.806.apk manually from the site.
  • It installs over your current version without uninstalling (same signature), so your data is kept.
  • Release details: Android 2.11.805 announcement.

Patch 2.11.6

Global chat is back, and up to 120 FPS.

Global chat is back

  • Open it from the chat (message) button on Home or from the lobby/matching screen.

Badges Are Back

2026-11-01

211 badges are now visible in the badge screen, and 131 of them can be earned today.

How to earn them

  • Play ranked or regular battles: wins (total, per kicker, per mode, solo and team), win streaks, MVP, kills, crystals, flags, Rapid Ball goals, ball drops, distance and likes.
  • Also counted: logins, disc upgrades and rank.

Good to know

  • Progress counts from now on. Past battles and logins are not counted retroactively.
  • Custom battles and Trial do not count.
  • Season, tournament and beta badges cannot be earned yet.

Scramble Mode & AI Roles

New matchmaking and more bot variety

2026-10-19

Better team composition in Scramble, Flag Flight, and Rapid Ball.

Team formation and bots

  • Matchmaking uses human player roles to guide team assignments, balances the number of human players on each team where possible, and keeps parties together.
  • When bots are needed, matchmaking fills open spots with complementary Kickers and varied Disc decks. Decks are selected to fit each bot's role, useful Disc effects, and that Kicker's cast times.
  • Bot builds include healing-focused options with Ruriha, traps with Owlbert, speed with Grenhawk, and damage support with Sid.
  • Combat AI behavior is unchanged.
